## Steps to Schedule a Post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Social Studio dashboard">
    ````
    Log in at runwaysocialstudio.com and select your property.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Create Post'">
    ````
    Choose the 'Create Post' button to start a new campaign.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your post content">
    ````
    Add text, images, and select which social channel to post on.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the post">
    ````
    Click 'Schedule', pick a date and time, then confirm.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
You’ll see a preview of scheduled posts on your dashboard calendar.
</Callout>
