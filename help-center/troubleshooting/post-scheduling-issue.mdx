---
title: Post Scheduling Not Working
description: How to resolve scheduled posts not appearing.
---

## Symptoms

- Scheduled posts are missing on Instagram or Facebook
- No notifications on post status

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm social account connection">
    ````
    Go to 'Settings' > 'Social Accounts'. Reauthorize if any account shows 'Disconnected.'
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check post time zone">
    ````
    Ensure the scheduled time matches your property’s time zone in settings.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Try rescheduling the post if issues persist.
</Callout>
