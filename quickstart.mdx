## Prerequisites

Before diving in, ensure you have the following ready:

<Callout kind="info">

- A valid email address for account creation
- Access to your social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X)
- Property details like address, photos, and amenities for profiles
- Admin access to your multifamily property management accounts

</Callout>

## Step 1: Create and Verify Your Account

Sign up quickly to access the dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Dashboard" icon="monitor">
    Navigate to `https://dashboard.runwaysocialstudio.com` and click **Sign Up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details" icon="user">
    Provide your email, create a strong password, and select **Multifamily Property Manager** as your role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for the verification link from `no-reply@runwaysocialstudio.com` and click it to activate your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use a business email like `manager@yourproperty.com` for team collaboration features.
</Callout>

## Step 2: Connect Your Social Media Channels

Link your property's social accounts to start posting. Runway supports major platforms.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Facebook" icon="facebook">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Authorize App">
        Click **Connect Facebook** in the dashboard, log in, and grant permissions for Pages management.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Page">
        Choose your property's Facebook Page (e.g., "Sunny Oaks Apartments Community").
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Instagram" icon="instagram">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Link via Facebook">
        Instagram requires Facebook login. Connect your linked Instagram Business account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify Ownership">
        Confirm you're the admin for the account associated with your property.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Twitter/X" icon="twitter">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Generate Token">
        In Twitter Developer Portal, create an app and get API keys.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Paste Credentials">
        Enter `API_KEY`, `API_SECRET`, `ACCESS_TOKEN`, and `ACCESS_SECRET` in Runway settings.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Step 3: Set Up Your Property Profiles

Define your communities to tailor content.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Property" icon="home">
    Go to **Properties > Add New**. Enter name (e.g., "Sunny Oaks Apartments"), address, and upload 5+ high-quality photos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Amenities" icon="settings">
    List features like pool, gym, pet-friendly policy, and resident events calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Posting Schedule" icon="calendar">
    Choose optimal times: Weekdays 9 AM-5 PM for engagement peaks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 4: Post Your First Content Update

Launch with a welcome post.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Post" icon="plus">
    Select your property, click **New Post**, and choose connected channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Content" icon="edit">
    Write: "Welcome to Sunny Oaks Apartments! 🌞 Join our community events and enjoy our resort-style pool. #MultifamilyLiving #ApartmentLife"
    
    Attach a photo from your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule & Publish" icon="send">
    Review preview, then **Publish Now** or schedule for peak time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
